# Honey Slide

A landscape iPhone/iPad math obstacle racer. Steer into the correct answer pot, avoid hives, steer around solid logs, and choose whether to take boosts or ramps.

Answer rows vary from two to five full-size pots. Upcoming pots and track items are prepared ahead of time and remain visible in the distance. The banks widen and narrow smoothly with the number of answers: two is narrower, three retains the original width, and four/five are wider. Stationary rows have short solid dividers. Moving rows have three pots bouncing together across a four-option-wide road without dividers.

Lightning gives three seconds of extra speed, animated honey whirlpools slow the rider for two seconds, and ramps carry the rider just over the answer row before landing immediately behind it. Ramps align with an answer divider. Obstacles and modifiers start at question two and are ready immediately when a saved level resumes. Difficulty reacts to correct streaks and repeated mistakes without rearranging already-visible rows.

Each correct answer earns one Honey for level progress and character unlocks. There is no separate points score, multiplier, star or bonus honey pickup. Paused sessions preserve progress and difficulty streaks; older saves remain compatible.

After the final answer row, the road funnels into one passage and ends at a springboard that spans exactly between the banks. The rider is guided to the center, launched completely out of view during a 0.6875-second flight, and only then the final score appears.
